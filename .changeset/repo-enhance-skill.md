Add the `repo` skill. `repo enhance` offers the vetted sections a repository's `AGENTS.md` is missing, one at a time,
and writes only what the owner approves. Its first section, Commit Discipline, has agents commit each finished unit of
work without waiting to be asked, with the `commit` skill. It goes in the repository's `AGENTS.md`, or is handed over
for the owner's global `~/.agents/AGENTS.md`. A section cyberplace's `init-commit-discipline` wrote is offered the new
wording as a replacement.
